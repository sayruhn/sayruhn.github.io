Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What we collect

When you connect TikTok, the app receives:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name, and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token, used only to upload drafts (video.upload) on your behalf.

How it is used

This data is used only to upload the videos you choose to your own TikTok account as drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored locally on the creator's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer. Videos are sent directly from the creator's computer to TikTok.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with TikTok, as required to perform uploads.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect at any time by revoking the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions). Deleting the app's local token file removes all stored data.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
